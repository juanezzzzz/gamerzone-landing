# COPY DIRECTION — Voice, Tone & Language

## Language

**Spanish** (Colombian casual). The site speaks in the user's voice, not corporate Spanish.

- "parche" not "grupo de amigos"
- "mecato" not "snacks"
- "puesto" not "estación de trabajo"
- "Nequi, Daviplata" not "medios de pago electrónicos"

The copy lives in `src/data/local.ts`. Do NOT change established copy without explicit request.

---

## Voice

**Confident peer.** GamerZone talks to you like a friend who knows their stuff, not a salesperson.

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| "Sin lag, sin excusas." | "Disfruta de la mejor experiencia de gaming con nuestros equipos de alta gama." |
| "Pagas por lo que jugaste." | "Ofrecemos tarifas competitivas y flexibles para todos los presupuestos." |
| "La revancha se cobra el mismo día." | "Vive momentos emocionantes de competición con tus amigos." |
| "Tu juego. Tu espacio." | "Una experiencia exclusiva y personalizada para ti y tus acompañantes." |

---

## Tone Per Section

| Section | Tone | Register |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | Cinematic, declarative | Short. Bold. One statement. |
| PC Arena | Technical, dry | Specs-forward. No fluff. |
| Console Lounge | Warm, social | Inviting. About the shared moment. |
| Futbol | Competitive, sporty | Direct. Challenge. Energy. |
| VIP | Exclusive, intimate | Minimal words. Let the space speak. |
| Statement | Philosophical, brand | The manifesto. One idea. |
| Tarifas | Clear, practical | No ambiguity. What you get, what you pay. |
| Cierre | Urgent, confident | One question. One CTA. |

## Display Headings

Display headings (Sora, massive scale) can mix Spanish and English when the English is gaming vocabulary that the audience already uses naturally:

- "PC ARENA" (not "ARENA DE PC")
- "CONSOLE LOUNGE" (natural gaming English)
- "PLAY TOGETHER" (natural)
- "VIP" (universal)
- "GAME PASS" (product name)

But body copy stays in Colombian Spanish. Never translate Colombian idioms into English.

---

## CTA Language

| Context | CTA Text | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Primary (WhatsApp) | "Reservar sesión" | Clear action, not vague |
| Secondary | "Escríbenos" | Conversational, low friction |
| VIP | "Reservar" | One word. Premium = minimal. |
| Final | "Reservar sesión" | Mirrors the primary for closure |

Never use: "Saber más", "Descubrir", "Explorar", "Conoce nuestros servicios", or other vague CTAs.

---

## Numbers & Data

- Prices use Colombian format: `$3.000 /h` (dot for thousands, no decimals)
- Specs use short form: `144 Hz`, `1 Gbps`, `50"`, `65"`
- Hours use 12h with Colombian convention: `2:00 p. m.`
- Counts are plain numbers: "7 puestos", not "siete puestos" (numbers as visual elements)

---

## What NOT to Write

- Corporate jargon ("experiencia integral", "soluciones de entretenimiento")
- Superlatives without substance ("el mejor", "la máxima experiencia")
- AI-sounding filler ("en un mundo donde...", "descubre un universo de...")
- Redundant descriptions of what the user can see in the photo
- Marketing buzzwords ("innovador", "revolucionario", "de vanguardia")
